Beyond Nine Mountains Panel

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

Beyond Nine Mountains Panel ("the tool") is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Beyond Nine Mountains to manage that channel. It is used by the channel owner only and does not sign in any other users.

Data the tool accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for these permissions on the owner's own YouTube account:

  • youtube.upload to upload the channel's videos;
  • youtube.force-ssl to set thumbnails and captions, schedule publishing, and read and answer comments on the channel's videos;
  • yt-analytics.readonly to read the channel's own statistics.

The tool also reads public data about the channel's videos (view, like and comment counts, public comments) through the YouTube Data API.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the channel's own videos and to show the owner how they perform and what viewers write. It is not used for advertising and it is not sold.

One transfer happens, and only to help the owner answer viewers: a public comment under the channel's video (its text and the commenter's public display name) may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which sorts out spam, drafts a reply and notes an episode idea if the viewer suggests one. The owner reads and edits every draft and decides whether to post it. Apart from this, the data is not shared with or transferred to anyone, and the tool does not use it to train any AI model.

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage

Sign-in tokens, statistics and comments are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on any other server. Public comments shown to the owner remain subject to YouTube's own terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account page Third-party apps with account access. The tool then stops working until it is connected again.
