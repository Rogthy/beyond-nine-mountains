Beyond Nine Mountains Panel

Beyond Nine Mountains is a YouTube channel about creatures from folklore and mythology: where each legend comes from, what people believed it did, and how they protected themselves from it.

Beyond Nine Mountains Panel is the private tool the channel uses to run itself. It signs in with Google only to work with the channel's own YouTube account, and it does three things:

  • uploads the channel's finished videos and Shorts, with their thumbnails and captions, and schedules when they go public;
  • reads the statistics of the channel's videos (views, likes, comments, subscribers) so the owner can see what works;
  • reads comments under the channel's videos so the owner can answer viewers.

The tool is used by the channel owner only. It is not offered to anyone else, it does not sign in other people, and it does not post anything the owner has not prepared.

How the tool handles data from Google is described in the privacy policy. Contact: rogthy.forge@gmail.com
